# Root Six Player

On Windows, open **Root Six Player.exe**. On Linux, extract the package and open **Root Six Player**. Root opens with a six-player menu, and the launcher exits. The mod handles hosting, saves, and returning to the menu. First-time setup automatically prepares isolated copies of your Steam installation. The launcher includes the mod and its setup dependencies. No Python installation is needed.

1. Install Root in Steam and keep Steam open.
2. Open the launcher. First-time setup can take a few minutes.
3. In Root, choose **Host a game**. Pick your faction, the other seats, and expansion options, then create the room. Click **Invite friends**, choose an open seat, and invite your friend. Start once everyone has joined. Friend seats can use Human or ordinary AI for any standard faction, with a shared Easy, Medium, or Hard difficulty. Clockwork factions use their separate rules.
4. To join, choose **Join friends**, then accept your host's invitation in Steam. Choose your faction in the waiting room and click **Join Game**.

Keep the host's computer on while playing. Matches save automatically. Active turn timers keep running if the host closes the game. Close Root to finish; the host can use **Resume a game** later and send new invitations.

Open this launcher before accepting an invitation, or Steam will open regular Root.

All gameplay expansions in the installed game are available in this private playtest.
Steam purchases and ordinary Root are unchanged. To report a problem, use **Copy diagnostics**
in **Match** or on the error screen. It copies versions and display/session details, without
saved games, chat, account names, or invitation secrets.
